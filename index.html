Exercise Execution Cards

Short, Actionable Tips

Upper Body & Push

DB Bicep Curls 5-8 reps
  • Keep elbows pinned to your sides; do not swing shoulders.
  • Squeeze biceps tightly at the top for 1 second.
  • Lower the dumbbell slowly (2-3 seconds down).
Knee Pushups 5-8 reps
  • Maintain a straight line from knees to head; brace core.
  • Keep elbows at a 45-degree angle relative to torso.
  • Chest almost touches floor on lower position.
Pike-to-Pushup / Pike Hold 5 reps / 10s hold
  • Hips high in upside-down V position; gaze towards feet.
  • Press floor firmly away through shoulders to lock out.
  • Lower head forward toward ground for pushup variation.

Core Control & Abs

Hollow Body Hold 15s hold
  • Press lower back completely flat into floor.
  • Lift shoulders and legs slightly off ground.
V Situps & Suitcases 10 reps each
  • Inhale down; exhale as chest and knees meet.
  • Controlled tempo—avoid using momentum.
Bicycle Crunches 10 slow reps
  • Slow twist; touch shoulder (not just elbow) to knee.
  • Pause 1 second on each side rotation.
Russian Twists 40 slow reps
  • Keep chest high; rotate ribs fully side-to-side.
  • Feet grounded if lower back feels strain.
Plank Hold & Plank Pushups 45s hold / 5 reps
  • Tuck pelvis slightly under (squeeze glutes) to shield lower back.
  • Push ground away through elbows; keep neck neutral.
